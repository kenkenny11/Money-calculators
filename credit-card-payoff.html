How long does it take to pay off a credit card with minimum payments?

Far longer than people expect. A 6,000 dollar balance at 24.99 percent APR with a 2 percent minimum takes over 20 years and costs more than 12,000 dollars in interest. Raising the payment to a fixed 200 dollars pays it off in about 3 years.

How is credit card interest calculated?

Most cards use daily interest: your APR divided by 365 is charged on your balance every day. At 24.99 percent APR, a 6,000 dollar balance grows by about 4 dollars a day, over 120 dollars a month, before your payment even counts.

What is the fastest way to pay off credit card debt?

Pay a fixed amount, never the minimum, and put every extra dollar at the highest APR card first (the avalanche method). A 0 percent balance transfer card can help if you can clear it during the intro period.

What if my payment is too small?

If your monthly payment does not even cover the monthly interest, the balance never shrinks. This calculator warns you when that happens.
