How much is car insurance per month?

The US average for full coverage is roughly 140 dollars per month (about 1,660 per year), but your actual rate swings hugely by state, age and driving record. A 45-year-old in Maine might pay 90. A 19-year-old in Michigan might pay over 400.

What drives your car insurance rate?

The five biggest factors: your state (Florida and Louisiana cost the most, Maine and Vermont the least), your age (under 25 can double the rate), your driving record, your credit profile (in most states), and your coverage level. Car value matters less than people think.

How can I pay less for car insurance?

Shop quotes from at least 3 insurers every year, rates for the exact same driver vary by over 1,000 dollars between companies. Also: raise your deductible, bundle with home or renters insurance, ask about low-mileage and good-student discounts.

Is this estimate exact?

No. It is a ballpark based on state averages and major rating factors. Insurers use dozens of variables. Use it to know what a fair price looks like before you get real quotes.
