Stamp Duty Calculator (UK)

England and Northern Ireland rates. First-time buyer and additional property aware.

How much is stamp duty in the UK?

In England and Northern Ireland, standard buyers pay nothing on the first 125,000 pounds, then 2 percent up to 250,000, 5 percent up to 925,000, 10 percent up to 1.5 million and 12 percent above. Only the slice of price inside each band pays that band rate.

What is the first-time buyer relief?

First-time buyers pay nothing up to 300,000 pounds and 5 percent on the slice up to 500,000. Above 500,000 the relief disappears and standard rates apply to the whole price.

What is the additional property surcharge?

Buying a second home or buy-to-let adds a 5 percent surcharge on top of the standard rates. Scotland uses LBTT and Wales uses LTT, which have different bands, so this calculator covers England and NI only.
